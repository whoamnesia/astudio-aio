# Conversion psychology for creatives

## 1. Awareness stage decides the message (Schwartz)
| Stage | They know | Lead with | Avoid |
|---|---|---|---|
| Unaware | nothing about the problem | a story/situation they recognise, curiosity | selling the product |
| Problem-aware | the pain | name the pain precisely in their words, then the way out | jargon, feature lists |
| Solution-aware | solutions exist | why this approach beats the others (mechanism, comparison) | generic benefits |
| Product-aware | you | proof, specifics, risk reversal, the offer | re-explaining basics |
| Most aware | ready | the offer, the deadline (if real), the button | long copy |
Retargeting audiences are usually product-/most-aware; cold audiences unaware/problem-aware.

## 2. Angles (generate many, ship the most different)
Pain ("Тратите 3 часа на отчёты?") · Desire/outcome ("Отчёт за 5 минут") · Proof ("4,8 из 5 — 2 140 отзывов", real) ·
Identity ("Для кофеен до 30 мест") · Contrarian ("Перестаньте делать рассылки по понедельникам") · Mechanism ("Сканирует
чек — сам раскладывает расходы") · Urgency (real deadline only) · Price/value ("Дешевле одного обеда в месяц") ·
Before/after (real results) · Social/FOMO (only verifiable) · Risk reversal ("14 дней бесплатно, без карты").

## 3. Hooks that stop the scroll
A face looking at the viewer (or at the CTA to direct gaze); the product in use or the result, not the logo; a pattern
interrupt (unexpected colour field, scale, crop, a question mark object); a big specific number; native-feeling formats
for the platform (creator-style for TikTok/Reels, clean editorial for LinkedIn); contrast with the feed around it
(if feeds are busy photos, a flat bold colour field stands out). First 0.5s in video; first glance in static.

## 4. Composition for conversion
- **Hierarchy:** focal visual → headline → CTA → proof → logo. Squint test must show this order.
- **Visual flow:** Z-pattern for landscape/square (logo top-left → headline → visual → CTA bottom-right), top-down for
  vertical; gaze and pointing lines (a person's eyes, an arrow made by the composition) lead to the CTA.
- **CTA:** highest-contrast element (Von Restorff), button shape, verb + outcome, ≥ 44px tall at 1080 wide scale,
  whitespace around it, positioned at the terminal area; one per banner.
- **Text economy:** headline ≤ 7 words, sub ≤ 12, total ≤ ~20 words on social; readable at 25% thumbnail.
- **Colour:** brand palette; the CTA colour reserved for the CTA across the set; avoid red/green-only meaning.
- **Faces and people:** real customers/team photos beat stock; if stock, avoid the obvious stock look; diversity where
  natural.
- **Trust elements:** real rating with source, guarantee, "без предоплаты", delivery time, a recognisable client logo only
  with permission.

## 4a. Accent details that catch the eye (use 2-3, never all)
A clean creative with no accent is easy to scroll past. Add **two or three** crafted details that pull the eye along
the path visual → headline → CTA, in the brand's style:
- **Sticker / badge** with the offer's key fact («0 ₽», «30 мин», «−20%» only if real): round or burst shape, slight
  rotation (−8…+8°), overlapping the visual's edge.
- **Highlighter marks**: a hand-drawn underline, circle or scribble (SVG path, brand colour) on the 1-2 key words;
  a marker-style highlight behind a number.
- **Directional cue**: a drawn arrow or the subject's gaze/gesture pointing at the CTA; a curved line linking
  headline and button.
- **Cut-out and depth**: the product or person cut out and breaking the frame edge, a soft real shadow, one object in
  the foreground overlapping the headline corner (never the words).
- **Contrast frame**: a coloured frame/inner border or a split background that makes the thumbnail pop in the feed.
- **Micro-texture**: grain, halftone dots or a pattern from the brand device on the background field (low contrast).
- **Big number**: the offer's number set huge as a graphic element.
Limits: the CTA stays the highest-contrast element; accents never cover text or the button; ≤ 3 accent devices per
creative; the same accent family across the set so the brand is recognisable; check at the 25% thumbnail that the
accents help the order of reading instead of fighting it.

## 5. Copy formulas
- **4U headline**: useful, urgent, unique, ultra-specific - score each candidate 1-4.
- **PAS** (problem → agitate → solve), **BAB** (before → after → bridge), **FAB** (feature → advantage → benefit).
- **Number + outcome + time**: "Сайт за 7 дней", "−30% к расходам за месяц" (true only).
- CTA verbs: Получить, Забронировать, Рассчитать, Скачать, Попробовать бесплатно, Записаться, Купить со скидкой; EN:
  Get, Book, Start free, Claim, Try, Download. Avoid «Подробнее», "Learn more", "Submit".
- Russian specifics: «вы» vs «ты» per brand; no канцелярит; numbers with spaces (2 140), ₽ after the number with NBSP.

## 6. Platform notes
- **Meta/Instagram:** 4:5 feed and 9:16 stories/reels first; text compact (heavy text reaches worse); keep stories' top
  250px and bottom ~340px clear; captions carry detail.
- **VK Ads:** 1080×1080 / 1080×1350 / 1080×607 and 1080×1920 stories; similar safe-zone logic.
- **Telegram Ads:** mostly text (+ media in some formats) - the headline and CTA wording is the creative; channel post
  images 1280×720.
- **TikTok:** native creator style, right 140px and bottom ~420px covered by UI; first second decides.
- **YouTube thumbnails:** big face/object + 3-5 words, readable at 168×94, bottom-right covered by the duration badge.
- **Google Display / Yandex RSYa:** many sizes - logo + 3-6 words + CTA; responsive display ads assemble assets
  (supply logo, images 1.91:1 and 1:1, short/long headlines); ≤ 150 KB for uploaded display banners.
- **Russia ad marking:** reserve space for «Реклама» + advertiser name/erid where the placement requires it.

## 7. Honesty line
No fake buttons/system dialogs, no fake countdowns, no invented reviews or "sold out" claims, no misleading before/after,
no implying endorsements. Compliance categories (finance, health, alcohol, gambling) have extra rules - flag them.
