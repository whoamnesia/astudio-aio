# A/B testing creatives

## Test matrix (one variable per test, in this order of impact)
1. **Angle/hook** (pain vs outcome vs proof vs identity) - biggest swings.
2. **Visual** (product vs person vs result vs type-only colour field).
3. **Headline** wording within the winning angle.
4. **CTA** wording, then button colour/shape.
5. **Format** (static vs carousel vs short video) and placement.
Keep the brand, offer and landing page constant within a test. 3-5 creatives per ad set; enough budget/time for
significance (rule of thumb: ≥ 1,000 impressions and ≥ 50-100 clicks per variant before judging CTR; conversions need
more). Kill clear losers, iterate on winners with a new single variable.

## Naming
`<campaign>_<angle>_<visual>_<headline-id>_<cta-id>_<format>-<WxH>.png`
e.g. `bean-spring_outcome_cup_h2_c1_ig-post-1080x1350.png`. Keep a table (angle, headline, sub, CTA, visual, sizes, file).

## What to measure
CTR (attention), CPC (cost of attention), landing CVR (message match), CPA/ROAS (the real goal), frequency and creative
fatigue (CTR decay over days → refresh the visual, keep the winning angle). A high-CTR / low-CVR creative usually
over-promises or mismatches the landing page - fix the message match, not just the banner.
