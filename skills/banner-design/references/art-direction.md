# Banner art direction

## 22 directions (pick by brand + purpose, then make it the brand's own)
1. **Minimal** - one focal element, 1-2 colours, lots of ground. SaaS, premium, announcements.
2. **Type-as-image** - oversized expressive headline fills the canvas. Launches, events, manifestos.
3. **Colour field** - one saturated brand field, flat, with ink type. Campaigns with a strong brand colour.
4. **Photo full-bleed** - one strong photograph, text in its calm region (or a scrim). Lifestyle, food, travel, real estate.
5. **Product hero** - the product large on a clean ground, price/CTA beside. E-commerce, launches.
6. **Duotone** - photo mapped to two brand colours. Bold brands, music, events.
7. **Geometric / construction** - shapes and grids from the logo's construction. Tech, architecture, finance.
8. **Editorial** - magazine grid, pull quote, serif or grotesk contrast. Media, luxury, culture.
9. **Collage / cut paper** - layered cut-outs, texture, tape. Youth, creative, food & craft.
10. **Illustrated** - a drawn scene in the brand's illustration style. Kids, education, friendly services.
11. **Data hero** - one real number as the image, with its context. Reports, fintech, B2B (true numbers only).
12. **Interface-as-picture** - a real product UI fragment cropped like a photo. Software.
13. **3D object** - a rendered/procedural object (astudio-aio:3d-elements still). Tech, product.
14. **Retro / print** - halftone, risograph palette, grain, misregistration. Events, music, craft.
15. **Swiss / International** - strict grid, flush-left type, one accent. Design-savvy B2B, culture.
16. **Neo-brutalist** - thick outlines, hard shadows, raw colour blocks (only for a brand that is this). Indie, Gen Z.
17. **Dark mood** - deep tinted ground, rich accent, high contrast. Premium, gaming, night events.
18. **Organic / nature** - earth tones, botanical texture, soft forms. Eco, wellness, food.
19. **Kinetic-ready** - layered elements designed to animate (GIF/MP4/HTML5 later). Social video covers.
20. **Pattern** - the brand's graphic device repeated as the field. Brand awareness, packaging-like.
21. **Before/after split** - two halves showing the change. Services, renovation, beauty (real photos only).
22. **Map / place** - the real map or street as the image. Local business, events, logistics.

## Composition
- **Three zones**: brand/value at the top or left, message + visual in the middle, CTA at the terminal area (bottom-right
  in LTR reading). Vertical formats stack; ultra-wide (8:1 leaderboard) runs logo → message → CTA left to right.
- **Focal point** first, then headline, then CTA, then logo - squint test must show that order.
- Re-compose per ratio family: square 1:1 / portrait 4:5 / vertical 9:16 / landscape 16:9 / wide 3:1+ / tiny display
  (320×50, 300×250). Each gets its own arrangement and type size, not a scaled copy.
- Text never over busy image areas; scrim (30-55% ink, local) when needed.
- Margins ≥ ~5% of the short side; YouTube channel art: everything vital inside the 1546×423 centre; story/reels: keep
  out of top 250px and bottom ~340px.

## Typography for banners
- Headline: 2-7 words, ≤ 3 lines, size from the canvas (≥ 6-10% of canvas height on social formats), tight tracking at
  large sizes, authored line breaks. Supporting line ≤ 12 words. CTA 1-3 words, action verb.
- Readability test: render at 25% size - headline and CTA still legible.
- Russian copy: longer words → condensed or narrower faces help; guillemets, NBSP after short words, no hanging
  prepositions at line ends.

## Imagery
Supplied photos first; free stock (Unsplash/Pexels/Pixabay, Openverse CC0) next with records; CSS/SVG-built visuals and
illustrations from the brand's device. Never AI-generated images. One image treatment across a set.
