# Brand strategy, voice and messaging

## 1. Strategy one-pager
```
MISSION       We [action] for [audience] by [method] so they can [outcome].
AUDIENCE      who, their situation, what they already believe, awareness stage
POSITIONING   For [target] who [need], [brand] is the [category] that [key benefit]. Unlike [alternative], we [differentiator].
PROMISE       one sentence the brand must keep everywhere
PROOF         real facts that make the promise believable (numbers, process, materials, guarantees, customers)
PERSONALITY   3 traits as "X, not Y" (confident, not arrogant · warm, not cute · precise, not cold)
NEVER         how the brand must never look or sound (and the category clichés it refuses)
```
Message testing: clear (no jargon)? differentiated (a competitor couldn't say it)? credible (provable)? compelling
(the audience cares)? consistent (aligns with the brand)?

## 2. Voice versus tone
Voice = the brand's consistent personality. Tone = how it adapts to the moment. Dimensions to place the brand on:
formal ↔ casual · simple ↔ technical · serious ↔ playful · reserved ↔ expressive.

**Voice chart**
| Trait | Means | Do | Don't |
|---|---|---|---|
| e.g. Direct | says the thing first | "Ships in 3 days." | "We strive to deliver in a timely manner." |

**Tone by context**
| Context | Tone shift | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Marketing headline | most confident, specific | |
| Product UI | plain, functional, verbs | "Save changes" |
| Error | calm, specific, helpful, never blaming | "Card declined. Try another card or contact your bank." |
| Success | brief, warm | "Published." |
| Support | empathetic, concrete next step | |
| Legal/billing | formal, exact | |

## 3. Vocabulary
- CTA verb list (the brand's names for its actions; one name per action everywhere).
- Words we use / words we never use (include the genre buzzwords and, for Russian brands, канцелярит and штампы -
  `reference/copy.md` §8).
- Number, date, currency and address formats for each locale (Russian: 1 500 ₽, 1 ноября 2026 г., +7 (999) 123-45-67).
- Address form: ты/вы for Russian (decide once; B2B and premium usually «вы», youth/consumer apps often «ты»);
  you/we in English.

## 4. Messaging architecture
Primary message (one sentence) → 3-5 supporting messages, each with the audience need it answers and its proof →
elevator pitches (10s / 30s / 60s) → message by audience segment (pain, message, CTA). Sample rewrites: three
before/after pairs turning generic copy into the brand voice.

## 5. Naming help (if asked)
Criteria: short, pronounceable in all target languages, spellable after hearing, no bad meanings in RU/EN/local
languages, domain/handle availability (check, do not assume), trademark search recommended. Generate across types:
descriptive, evocative, invented, compound, metaphor, founder/place. Present 8-12 with one-line rationale; flag risks.
